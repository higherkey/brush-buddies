# Toothpaws — Character Bible

These are the canonical storytelling profiles for all 75 buddies. Every character has a distinct temperament, lived history, place, motivation, and gentle growth challenge. The data used by the app lives in [`characters.js`](characters.js). Environment names and adventure stops are defined by `buddies.js` and `adventure-data.js`; do not change them here without updating the in-app routes.

**Story principles:** Warm, imaginative, readable aloud to young children; problems are small, feelings are respected, helpers are capable, and each resolution comes from the character's choices. Keep settings and names stable across future chapters. No reward should require extra brushing beyond the normal timer.

## Toebean the Cat (Tobee)

- **Personality:** Gentle, observant, and stubbornly kind.
- **Background:** She learned to mend neighbors' worn belongings from Aunt Mallow.

- **Growth edge:** She tries to solve every problem without asking for help.

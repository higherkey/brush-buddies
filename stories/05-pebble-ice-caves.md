# Pebble and the Sparkly Ice

**Buddy:** Pebble the Penguin  
**Destination:** Pebble's Ice Caves  
**Stops:** Ice Slide → Fish Pond → Igloo Door  
**Goal item:** Sparkly Ice  
**Read-aloud time:** About 5 minutes

Pebble the Penguin had a plan.

His little sister was afraid of the dark, and tonight was the winter lantern festival. Pebble wanted to make her a special lantern that would sparkle like a star, even when the sky was cloudy.

His grandfather had told him about a piece of **Sparkly Ice** that sometimes formed near the Ice Caves.

“It catches the light in a hundred tiny colors,” Grandfather said. “But you must look carefully. Not every glitter is what it seems.”

Pebble tucked a small lantern into his scarf and waddled into the snowy morning.

His first stop was the **Ice Slide**.

The slide curved between two smooth blue hills. Pebble loved sliding, but today he needed to look for the sparkling ice.

He climbed to the top, sat down, and pushed off.

Wheeeee!

He slid so fast that his scarf streamed behind him like a flag.

At the bottom, something glittered.

Pebble hurried over and picked it up.

It was a silver fish scale.

“Very shiny,” he said, “but not quite right.”

A seal pup nearby clapped her flippers.

“That was a wonderful slide! Could you show me how to do it? I'm nervous about going so fast.”

Pebble looked toward the caves. He had a long way to go.

Then he smiled. “We can start slowly.”

He showed the pup how to sit, balance, and push off gently. They took three little slides together, each one a bit longer than the last.

By the end, the pup was laughing.

“Thank you!” she called. “Look for the rainbow reflections near the pond!”

Pebble continued to the **Fish Pond**.

The pond was covered with clear ice except for a small opening where fish swam below. A pale rainbow danced across its surface.

“Sparkly Ice!” Pebble cried.

He crouched and looked closer.

The colors came from sunlight passing through a hanging icicle. It was lovely, but the icicle was too thin to carry safely.

Pebble sighed.

Then he heard a splash.

A small fish was trapped in a shallow pocket of water, separated from the deeper pond by a ridge of slush.

“Can you help?” the fish asked.

Pebble used his flippers to push the loose slush aside. Soon the fish could swim back to its family.

“Thank you!” the fish said. “The cave entrance glitters at sunset. Perhaps that's what you're looking for.”

Pebble hurried toward the **Igloo Door**, the round entrance to the Ice Caves.

By now the sun was sinking. Pink and gold light stretched across the snow.

Pebble searched beside the door. He looked beneath a drift, behind a stone, and inside a little hollow.

Nothing.

He sat down, feeling tired.

“Maybe I won't find it,” he whispered.

Just then, the last sunbeam slipped through the round doorway and struck a tiny blue crystal embedded in the ice beside his foot.

It flashed pink, green, gold, and violet.

Pebble gasped.

He had found the **Sparkly Ice**!

He carefully loosened the small piece and carried it home wrapped in his scarf.

That evening, he placed it in a little lantern where it could catch the warm glow of a safe, covered light.

His sister watched the colors dance across the walls.

“It's like having our own stars,” she whispered.

Pebble sat beside her.

“Would you like to hear how I found it?”

He told her about the slide, the seal pup, the fish, and the sunset at the Igloo Door.

His sister listened with wide eyes.

“Were you ever scared?” she asked.

“A little,” Pebble admitted. “But I kept going. And I had help.”

She snuggled closer.

Outside, the winter wind sang softly around the igloo. Inside, the little lantern shone.

And when the room grew dark, Pebble's sister knew there was always a little light to be found.

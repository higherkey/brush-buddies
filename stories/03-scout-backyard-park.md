# Scout and the Bouncy Ball Mystery

**Buddy:** Scout the Puppy  
**Destination:** Scout's Backyard Park  
**Stops:** Ball Field → Bone Burrow → Splashy Puddle  
**Goal item:** Bouncy Ball  
**Read-aloud time:** About 5 minutes

Scout the Puppy believed there were three wonderful things in the world: breakfast, friends, and chasing a ball.

Not necessarily in that order.

One sunny morning, Scout invited his friends to the backyard park for the Great Big Bounce-Off. The rules were simple: everyone would take turns bouncing Scout's favorite green ball, and the funniest bounce would win.

Scout trotted to the **Ball Field** to begin.

“Ready, everybody?” he barked.

His friends cheered.

Scout reached into his toy basket.

No ball.

He tipped the basket upside down. Out fell a rope, a squeaky duck, two sticks, and a sock that nobody wanted to claim.

But no green ball.

“Oh no,” Scout said. “We can't have a Bounce-Off without the bouncy ball!”

His friend Daisy the Deer examined the grass.

“Look,” she said. “There's a round mark here, and another over there.”

Scout sniffed the marks. The ball had bounced away!

They followed the marks past the fence and toward the **Bone Burrow**, a small sandy hollow where Scout liked to hide his treasures.

Scout dug with his front paws.

Scritch-scratch! Scritch-scratch!

He uncovered a chewy bone, a smooth stone, and a second chewy bone that he had completely forgotten about.

“Excellent!” Scout said, wagging his tail.

“Focus,” Daisy reminded him.

“Right. Ball first. Bones later.”

They found a trail of little paw prints beside the burrow. Scout sniffed them.

“Those aren't mine,” he said.

A tiny voice came from behind a flowerpot.

“I didn't mean to take it.”

A young raccoon stepped out, twisting his paws together.

“I saw the green ball bounce past,” he said. “I tried to catch it for you, but it bounced out of my hands and rolled down the hill.”

Scout's ears drooped. The raccoon looked as if he expected to be scolded.

“Did you get hurt?” Scout asked.

“No.”

“Then it's all right,” Scout said. “Let's find it together.”

The raccoon brightened and joined them.

At the bottom of the hill lay the **Splashy Puddle**.

It was enormous after last night's rain. Frogs sat along its edges, and the sky reflected so clearly in the water that it looked like a second, upside-down sky.

Something green floated near the middle.

“My ball!” Scout shouted.

He dashed toward the puddle.

SPLASH!

Water flew everywhere. Daisy leaped backward. The raccoon hid behind a reed. Scout emerged with a lily pad stuck to his head.

The green thing was not the ball.

It was a frog's favorite floating leaf.

“Sorry!” Scout said, handing it back.

They searched the muddy edge. Then the raccoon noticed something bobbing beneath a fallen branch.

“There!”

It was the **Bouncy Ball**, trapped against the wood.

Scout stretched out one paw, but the ball floated away. Daisy held the branch steady while the raccoon nudged the ball toward the bank with a stick.

Scout caught it between his paws.

“Hooray!”

They hurried back to the Ball Field, a little damp and very muddy.

The Bounce-Off began at last.

Daisy's bounce was graceful. The raccoon's bounce was surprisingly high. Scout's bounce hit a tree, flew backward, and landed right in his water bowl.

SPLOOSH!

Everyone laughed, especially Scout.

“And the winner,” Scout announced, “is everybody!”

He let the young raccoon take the first turn of the next round.

That evening, Scout washed his paws and put the ball safely in the basket.

Then he paused.

Perhaps tomorrow, he thought, they could have a Great Big Splash-Off instead.

He was quite certain his friends would love that too.

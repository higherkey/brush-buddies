# Bubbles and the Lucky Clover

**Buddy:** Bubbles the Bunny  
**Destination:** Bubbles' Clover Burrow  
**Stops:** Carrot Patch → Clover Hill → Cozy Burrow  
**Goal item:** Lucky Clover  
**Read-aloud time:** About 5 minutes

Bubbles the Bunny woke to the sound of rain tapping on the roof of her burrow.

Tap-tap-tap. Pitter-patter. Plink!

“Today's the picnic!” she gasped.

Every spring, the meadow animals gathered for a picnic. Bubbles had promised to bring something special: a lucky four-leaf clover to place in the middle of the picnic blanket.

Her grandmother had once told her that lucky clovers grew where the morning dew lingered longest.

“Then I know where to begin,” Bubbles said.

She put on her yellow rain boots and hopped to the **Carrot Patch**.

The carrots stood in neat rows, their leafy tops trembling in the drizzle. Bubbles checked between the rows for clovers. She found a beetle, a shiny pebble, and one very muddy worm.

“Good morning,” said the worm. “Why are you turning over every leaf?”

“I'm searching for a lucky clover,” said Bubbles.

“Ah,” said the worm. “Try looking where the grass grows high. But first, could you help? The rain has washed dirt over my tunnel.”

Bubbles glanced toward the hill. She was already worried about being late.

Then she set down her basket and gently scooped the wet soil away.

The worm wriggled into his tunnel. “Thank you! I hope your luck finds you.”

At **Clover Hill**, the rain stopped.

The hill shimmered with thousands of tiny green leaves. Three leaves here. Three leaves there. Everywhere Bubbles looked, she saw three.

She searched beside a stone, beneath a daisy, and around an old tree stump.

Nothing.

A gust of wind suddenly whisked her picnic invitation from her basket.

“Oh, carrots!” Bubbles cried.

She chased the paper across the hill until it caught on a thorny bush. A small bird was perched nearby, looking worried.

“My nest fell down in the storm,” the bird chirped. “I can't carry it back alone.”

Bubbles could see the picnic meadow in the distance. Her friends would be arriving soon.

But the nest was right there, and the bird needed help.

“Let's fix it,” she said.

Bubbles carried the nest carefully to a low branch. The bird tucked it into place and sang a bright, grateful song.

When Bubbles looked down, she noticed a patch of clover beneath the tree. She counted the leaves.

One, two, three.

She sighed.

“Maybe there isn't a lucky clover today.”

She returned to her **Cozy Burrow** to dry her boots and gather her picnic food.

Inside, everything smelled of warm bread and fresh carrots. Bubbles put her basket on the table. She had almost forgotten about the clover when she heard a knock.

It was the worm, with the bird perched on a branch behind him.

“We brought something,” said the worm.

The bird held a small green stem in her beak.

Bubbles leaned closer.

One leaf. Two leaves. Three leaves. Four!

“A **Lucky Clover**!”

“We found it near my tunnel,” the worm explained. “We thought it belonged with someone who stopped to help.”

Bubbles accepted it carefully, but then she looked at her two new friends.

“Would you like to come to the picnic?”

The bird chirped yes. The worm nodded, which is quite an impressive thing for a worm to do.

They all went together.

At the picnic, Bubbles placed the clover in the middle of the blanket. Everyone admired its four perfect leaves.

“Do you think it will bring us luck?” asked a squirrel.

Bubbles looked around at the animals sharing food, laughter, and stories.

“I think,” she said, “we've already found the lucky part.”

And for the rest of the afternoon, the meadow felt wonderfully full of good fortune.
